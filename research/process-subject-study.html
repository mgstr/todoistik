The processing screen, and where the eye lands

The screen lost its crumb line (“Inbox Zero · 4 left”) and its “What is it?” heading, and its eight stacked forms became rows of buttons. What is left is a captured sentence set as an ordinary heading, sitting above four controls that are visually louder than it is — the buttons have borders and fills, the subject has nothing. This page renders the whole screen five ways, so the comparison is the real screen and not a swatch.

The run counter is a separate question

Removing the crumb also removed the only place a run said how much was left, and the nav badge is suppressed while the screen is up (implementation.md, “Navigation”). So a run now counts down invisibly. The three toggles above put the count back in the nav, put it on the subject as a 4 left chip, or leave it gone. Worth deciding on its own: it is orthogonal to A–E, and “gone” is a defensible answer — a count you cannot see is also a count you cannot be discouraged by.
