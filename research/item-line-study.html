Item Line Study

Two decisions in one page: how an item and its age sit on a line, and what words the age is written in. Both apply to every list in the app, not just the Inbox — the row template is shared.

Short items flatter every layout. The long ones are where the right-hand column earns or loses its place.

Same position as B, but the age is set in monospace, uppercase, letterspaced. The change of typeface is what does the separating — it stops being words in the sentence and becomes a stamp on it, without needing a box, a colour or a rule to say so. It also matches the `g` hints and the key bar, which already use mono for "this is the machine talking, not your text".

Flag 1 — two days has no rule

Your scale goes yesterday at one day, then k days ago starting at three. Nothing covers an age of two days. The fix is to start the range at two, which is what the table above assumes — unless you meant "2 days ago" to read as something else.

Flag 2 — "a month ago" and "2 months ago" leave a gap

a month ago was to end at 8 weeks (56 days) and k months ago to begin at 2 months (about 61). Days 57 to 60 fall between them.

Cleanest fix is to let "a month ago" run to 59 days, so it ends exactly where "2 months ago" begins — which also matches how it reads: anything not yet two months old is still "a month ago".

One more wording call. You wrote "week ago", "month ago", "year ago". The table uses a week / a month / a year, which reads better beside "3 weeks ago" and costs nothing. Say if you want the bare form.

Months are counted as 30 days, and years as 365. Calendar-accurate arithmetic would make "2 months ago" mean different spans in different seasons for no gain — this is a rounded label, not a date.
