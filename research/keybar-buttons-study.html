Six ways to make a key bar entry pressable

Every button on a form now has a key, so the buttons come off the forms and the bar becomes the one place a control is pressed — by keyboard first, by pointer when a pointer is what is in your hand. This page settles only what a bar entry looks like once it is a button. Each variant is rendered with style.css itself, in both themes, and again in a narrow frame, because the bar scrolls sideways rather than wrapping and a wider entry is the price every one of these pays.

The screen over each bar is an action's page with its seven-button row already removed. That removal is the change, and it is the same in every variant.

The one rule a treatment has to keep

The bar's existing promise is that everything it lists works right now (keys.md, “What a key is”). Making entries pressable adds a second thing it must not lie about: an entry is a button exactly when it presses a control. Movement and prefixes — j k move, g go to — are how you steer, not things you press, and nothing below may paint them as pressable. That is why every variant is shown against a bar holding both kinds.

AThe bar as it stands

The reference. Quiet chrome, read at a glance and never aimed at. Here so the cost of every other variant is visible as a difference from this.

Cost: none, and no gain — there is nothing to press.

BNothing at rest, a pill under the pointer

Identical to A until the pointer arrives, then the entry under it lifts into a tinted pill. The bar stays the quiet strip it was designed as, and the buttons are found the way a hover target is found anywhere — by moving across the row you already know is the controls.

Cost: at rest nothing says which entries are pressable, so on a touch screen or a first read the answer is “try one”. Gain: the only variant that costs the keyboard reading nothing at all.

CA chip at rest

Every pressable entry is a bordered pill; movement and prefixes stay bare text. The bar becomes visibly two kinds of thing, which is exactly what it now is. Delete keeps the danger colour it wore on the form.

Cost: the widest of the calm variants — a border and its padding on every entry, which is what tips the crowded bar into scrolling. Gain: the two kinds are told apart without hovering, in a chip shape the app already owns.

DThe letter as a keycap

The letter is drawn as a key rather than merely coloured as one, and the whole entry is the target. The bar reads as a picture of the keyboard, which is what it is — the pointer is the second way in and looks like it.

Cost: a cap on the unpressable entries too, so the two kinds are still only told apart on hover; decoration on a strip that had none. Gain: the key, not the word, is what the eye lands on — which is the habit the bar exists to build.

EA segmented bar

Full-height cells with hairline dividers, the way a toolbar divides. The target is the whole cell rather than the words in it — the largest target here at a given width. Entries that press nothing are not cells; they sit in the gaps between them, so the cells are the controls.

Cost: the loudest at rest — a row of dividers under every screen in the app. Gain: the biggest targets, and the two kinds separated structurally rather than by paint.

FThe button row, moved

The literal reading of the change: the buttons did not go away, they went down into the bar, keeping the styling they had on the form — Save primary, Delete danger — shrunk to bar size and carrying their letter.

Cost: a filled accent button under every screen competes with the view for the one loud thing on it, and the bar stops being chrome. Gain: nothing has to be re-learnt — the row that was over the fold is the row under it.

What this page does not settle. Two consequences of taking the buttons off the forms are real whichever paint wins, and both are decisions rather than pixels:

— ^m hangs its letters on the controls of the open screen, and most of what it hangs them on today are these buttons. With the buttons in the bar it has boxes and lists left, and the six letters keys.md pins under “Buttons that get no letter” (n x p u i c) need somewhere to live — the bar entry itself is the obvious hook.

— the bar is derived from the page: it lists a key only because it found the control that key presses. Once the control is no longer on the page, the bar has to become the place the control lives rather than a reading of it. That is a change to what the bar is, not to how it looks.
