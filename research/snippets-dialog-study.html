The snippets under the digits

On an edit or create screen the nine digits stop being bookmarks and become snippets: ^3 writes a kept run of names into the meta line, and ^0 is the nine of them on the screen. This page is about that second key — what the dialog that keeps them looks like, in the app's own stylesheet, in both themes.

Four shapes, and they differ on one question: is this a list you read and press, like the bookmarks, or a form you type in? Everything else follows from that answer.

2026-09-26 — B was chosen. It is the bookmarks dialog, and being the bookmarks dialog is the whole of the argument: one shape for nine numbered slots, one set of keys to remember, and a bare digit that still means the slot it is printed beside.

The specimens use the real palette, the real 7.5rem gutter and the real key bar.

The four

A

Nine boxes

the bookmarks dialog with every row already open

Nine token boxes stacked under a digit each, all live at once. Nothing to open, nothing to close: you arrow into the one you want and type. Each writes when it loses the focus, so there is no Save — the same way the panel chooser posts on the press.

What it costs: the digits go dead inside the dialog — a 1 pressed in here is a character, not slot one — and so do j and k, because the caret is always in a box. Nine inputs is also nine completion lists and nine places the wavy underline can be, on a dialog that is a third full most of the time.

B

Read down, open one — chosen

the bookmarks dialog's keyboard, unchanged

The nine read as text, exactly as the bookmarks do, and one of them opens into a box when you press it. j k move, ↵ opens the row under the cursor, a bare digit opens that slot from anywhere in the list, ⌫ empties one and esc closes — first the box, then the dialog. One box is open at a time, so there is one completion list and one place the caret can be.

It is the same dialog you already know, because it is the same dialog: a numbered list of nine kept things, where the digit is the whole address and the empty slots are part of the answer.

What it costs: editing a slot is two presses rather than none, and the row changes height as it opens.

C

A form, in the app's own shape

nine gutter rows and a Save

If it is a form, draw it like every other form in the app: the .stack, the 7.5rem label gutter, the box taking what is left, ^↵ to submit. The digit is the label, the way Title and Meta are labels one screen over.

What it costs: a Save, which the bookmarks deliberately do not have — and the gutter is 7.5rem of nothing to hold a single character, so the boxes start a third of the way across a dialog that is mostly boxes. It reads as a settings screen that happens to be floating.

D

A section on Settings

no dialog at all — ^0 goes there

Snippets are configuration, and the app already has one screen for the configuration that is neither a screen nor an item. Nine rows there, reached with g e like anything else on it, and ^0 is simply the jump.

What it costs: the thing this whole feature exists for is a half-written action with unsaved edits in it, and ^0 would walk away from it. That is the argument against, and it is the whole argument: the bookmarks dialog is a dialog for exactly this reason — you look something up without leaving what you were doing.

What the two open questions were answered with

What may a snippet hold — the narrow answer or the wide one?

The wide one, minus the dates. Everything an action's meta line carries by name, the sizes and #today included, because those are exactly the things typed over and over. Not due: or snooze:: a slot is stamped on many items and a moment belongs to one, so due:2026-09-20 under a digit is wrong the day after it was written. ParseSnippet in tokens.go is the refusal; BOX_RULES.snippet is the box saying so as you type.

And the collision with the bookmarks

The digits belong to the line the screen has. A filter line means bookmarks, a meta line means snippets, and no screen has both — so it is decided by the screen and never by a mode. Nothing is lost: a bookmark's going half is g 1…g 9 and works from an edit screen as it does everywhere, and its keeping half needs a filter line, which an edit screen has none of.
